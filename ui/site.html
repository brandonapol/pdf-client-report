Client Weekly Report

A ChatGPT plugin that turns this week's metrics CSV into a one-page Friday client report: what moved, what to do next, and a PDF you review before it goes out.

MCP endpoint

https://pdf-client-report-6it6p.ondigitalocean.app/mcp

How it works

  1. Ask ChatGPT for this week's client report and attach a metrics CSV.
  2. The plugin ranks week-over-week movers from the numbers you gave it. It never guesses a missing baseline.
  3. You review the headline, what moved, and the next actions, and correct anything.
  4. It renders a one-page PDF and gives you a download link that expires after 15 minutes.

CSV format

metric and value are required. previous is what makes a change real.

metric,value,previous,unit,note
Paid search sessions,1840,1210,sessions,Brand campaign launched Tuesday
Email clicks,210,260,clicks,Send went out late

Your data

The CSV is processed in memory. Each rendered PDF is held in memory for 15 minutes behind an unguessable link, and nothing is written to disk. See the privacy policy.
